## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where are my sessions stored?**  
Sessions are stored as TOML files in `%APPDATA%\Baudly` on Windows. macOS and Linux paths are TBC.

**Where are my macros stored?**  
Macros are stored in the browser's localStorage within the Tauri WebView. They persist between restarts automatically.

**Does Baudly store my passwords?**  
Passwords and SSH key passphrases are stored securely in the Windows Credential Manager. They are never written to disk in plain text.

**Is Baudly free to use?**  
Baudly is commercial proprietary software by GreenChapel Dev. A personal/trial license is available. Commercial use requires a paid license — contact sales@baudly.dev for pricing.
